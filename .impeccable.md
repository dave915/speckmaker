# Design Context

Audience: Designers and developers reviewing HTML prototypes together. Confirmed by the user.
Use cases: Upload mockups by project; unfold HTML tabs into separate screens; compare on a zoomable canvas; leave pinned discussions and see collaborators.
Personality: Calm, precise, collaborative. Figma-like working surface, confirmed by the user.
Direction: Crisp light studio chrome, pale dotted canvas, charcoal controls and a restrained citron accent. No marketing landing page. Manrope for the wordmark and numbers, Noto Sans KR for Korean interface copy.
